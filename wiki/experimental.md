# Experimental

***Warning: WIP, less tested, disabled by default***

Each entry can be switched on in the config, under `Experimental`.

## Duos

- **Convective Vortex** (Ares × Hestia) · `ConvectiveVortex` — Your Blade Rift lasts 50% longer and inflicts 25 Scorch per hit. Scorch on foes in the rift builds up instead of burning down.
  - Meat Grinder
  - Flame Strike, Flame Flourish, Smolder Ring
- **Goddess Spite** (Aphrodite × Hera) · `GoddessSpite` — After you take damage from an attack, inflict the attacker with Hitch and deal more damage back to all foes with Hitch (up to 9999).
  - Dying Wish, Hereditary Bane, Rousing Reception *(BoonEdits)*
  - Flutter Strike, Flutter Flourish
- **Volcanic Crown Alt** (Hephaestus × Hestia) · `VolcanicCrown` — Your Omega Cast erupts in a large Hepahestus blast and 6 fireballs, leaving burning embers behind.
  - Volcanic Strike, Volcanic Flourish
  - Glowing Coal, Controlled Burn, Volcanic Crown *(BoonEdits)*
- **Wrathful Hubris** (Hera × Ares) · `WrathfulHubris` — You deal 50% more total damage, but take 50% more total damage. Total damage is applied after all other damage modifiers.
  - Sworn Strike, Sworn Flourish, Engagement Ring, Nexus Rush, Born Gain
  - Any core boon from Ares
- **Glowing Brand** (Hephaestus × Hestia) — Hestia's fireballs inflict Glow. Your Glow is twice as potent and lasts twice as long.
  - Furnace Blast, Anvil Ring *(BoonEdits)*, Smithy Rush *(BoonEdits)*
  - Glowing Coal, Controlled Burn, Volcanic Crown *(BoonEdits)*

## Alternate versions

Turning one of these on replaces the regular duo in the pool.

- **Intertwined Fate Alt** (Aphrodite × Hera) · `IntertwinedFateAlt` — All foes are Hitched and share their status curses with each other. You deal 5% more damage for each foe with Hitch.
  - Dying Wish, Hereditary Bane, Rousing Reception *(BoonEdits)*
  - Flutter Strike, Flutter Flourish
- **Plasmic Capacitor Alt** (Ares × Zeus) · `PlasmicCapacitorAlt` — Your Blitz leaves Plasma behind, which creates chain-lightning when collected. Maximum plasma cap raised to 120.
  - Grisly Gain, Visceral Impact, Meat Grinder *(BoonEdits)*, Profuse Bleeding *(BoonEdits)*
  - Heaven Strike, Heaven Flourish
- **Solar Eclipse Alt** (Apollo × Ares) · `SolarEclipseAlt` — Move and strike 10% faster for each Dazed foe, up to 50%. If all foes are Dazed, go Dark and deal double damage for 5 seconds (40 second recharge).
  - Solar Ring, Blinding Rush, Dazzling Display, Light Smite
  - Grievous Blow, Mutual Destruction, Blood Spree
- **Storm Front Alt** (Zeus × Demeter) · `StormFrontAlt` — Demeter's Gusts are larger and rapidly inflict Blitz instead of throwing chain lightning. Blitz internal cooldown reduced.
  - Arctic Gale, Frigid Rush
  - Heaven Strike, Heaven Flourish
- **Untouchable Glow Alt** (Apollo × Aphrodite) · `UntouchableGlowAlt` — +10% chance to Dodge. All your damage to nearby foes is increased by your chance to avoid their attacks, counting your Dodge chance and their Daze (30% chance → 30% more damage).
  - Flutter Strike, Flutter Flourish, Glamour Gain
  - Solar Ring, Blinding Rush, Dazzling Display, Light Smite
- **Winter Solstice Alt** (Apollo × Demeter) · `WinterSolsticeAlt` — Channelling Omega moves creates a large Gust that Chills foes, and your Freeze and Chill effects make foes 50% slower.
  - Ice Strike, Ice Flourish, Arctic Ring, Frigid Rush, Tranquil Gain
  - Nova Strike, Nova Flourish, Solar Ring, Blinding Rush, Lucid Gain
